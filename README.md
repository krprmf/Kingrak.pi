### About Me

I'm a **Data Scientist & AI-Augmented Developer & Product Owner** from Bangkok, Thailand.

M.Sc. Computer Engineering @ Chula | B.Eng. Electrical Engineering @ Chula

My journey started with a curiosity for **data** — exploring patterns, building pipelines, and turning raw numbers into something meaningful. That foundation pulled me deeper into **machine learning and AI**: training models, fine-tuning transformers, engineering prompts, and figuring out how to actually put these tools into production.

But the more I built, the more I noticed that the hardest part isn't the algorithm — it's understanding what people genuinely need. That realization gradually shifted my focus toward the **intersection of customer voice and engineering delivery**. I started spending as much energy listening to users and stakeholders as I did writing code — shaping backlogs, pushing back on scope creep, and making sure the team solves the right problem before touching the keyboard.

Today I bridge both worlds: I bring enough technical depth to speak dev, and enough product sense to speak human.

Python is still my go-to for prototyping and backend work; for everything else, I ship fast with AI tools.

**Currently:** Data Scientist @ [VEKIN](https://vekin.tech/) (Deep Tech for Sustainability) -- bridging product and engineering on the DEMP platform.

**Into:** Product ownership, stakeholder communication, LLM prompt engineering, and AI-augmented development.
